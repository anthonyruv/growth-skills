## What you get

A complete page outline and copy draft, with a primary call to action, a claim-to-evidence checklist, and one suggested first test. If you ask your coding agent to build it, the skill can also guide implementation in your existing project.

## When to use it

Use it for a new sales page, an unclear hero section, or an existing page that explains many features but leaves the buyer unsure what they are getting.

## What to bring

Share the offer, intended buyer, traffic source, desired action, and real proof. Include an existing page or design direction when you have one. The skill asks for gaps that affect the promise and marks any remaining assumptions.

## How it works

1. Make the offer understandable: who it is for, what it is, and what happens next.
2. Connect the promise to a delivery plan and relevant proof.
3. Keep the strongest useful details; remove repetition and distracting claims.
4. Draft the page and identify one important uncertainty to test.

> A proof gap stays a proof gap. The skill never fills it with invented testimonials, customer logos, or results.

## Try it

> Use build-landing-page for our bookkeeping offer. The page will receive referrals from design studio owners. The goal is to book a 20-minute fit call. We have a real sample report but no customer testimonials. Draft the page and explain where the sample should appear.

The result should make the service concrete, use the sample as relevant evidence, and carry one clear booking action through the page.
