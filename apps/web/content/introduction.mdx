## A practical library

Growth Skills turns selected business lessons into reusable instructions for an AI agent. Each skill focuses on a job and produces an artifact you can inspect, edit, and use.

The first collection connects three jobs: shape an offer, turn it into a landing page, and plan an experiment. You can use them independently.

## Choose a starting point

| Your situation                               | Start here                                             |
| -------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| The offer is difficult to explain            | [Create an offer](/skills/create-offer)                |
| You know what you sell and need a page       | [Build a landing page](/skills/build-landing-page)     |
| You have a page and need to learn what works | [Plan a conversion test](/skills/plan-conversion-test) |

## What a skill contains

A skill is a folder with a `SKILL.md` instruction file and focused source notes. Your agent reads the instructions when you ask it to do the relevant job. The files are portable and editable.

## Keep your judgment

Skills help structure work. They do not create customer evidence, guarantee conversion, or replace validation with actual buyers. You remain in control of what gets published and tested.

This is an independent project informed by selected public teaching. Read [Sources & method](/docs/sources) for the precise review coverage, then [install the collection](/docs/installation).
